**What changed in the paper since v2.1** (audit log Entries 39–58). The changes are to wording only, and no estimate changed:

- The paper is rewritten in the first person following Strunk and White and Zinsser, with §5–§6 restructured.
- Box 1 defines the terms used.
- The abstract is now a plain-language summary of about 290 words, in three paragraphs. It opens with the question and its answer: on the 1999–2019 evidence, no. The best estimate is that adding credit made GDP forecasts 10.6% worse, with a post-lock sensitivity interval from about 6% better to 44% worse.
- Abstract phrasing is replaced by concrete statements throughout.
- "A recovery that never came" becomes "a sharp rebound that did not materialise".
- The methods lesson on power and coverage is scoped to this predictor path and simulated process.

The locked headline sentence, the final reading, the pre-written pandemic-period sentences and all ten tables are unchanged, as a script check confirms.
